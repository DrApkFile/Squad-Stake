Five friends are saving for
a trip.
One of them collects everyone’s money.
Now one person carries all
the risk —
and everyone else just has
to trust them.
That’s how group saving works
almost everywhere.
Someone holds the pot.
Nobody else can check it.
And while it waits,
the money does nothing.
If the holder spends it,
disappears, or simply makes a
mistake, the whole group loses.
So we built Squad Stake,
a mini app inside Nimiq Pay.
Save together for anything —
and nobody holds the pot.
Every member’s share stays in
their own wallet,
staked on the Nimiq blockchain,
earning while the squad waits.
And the squad’s progress isn’t
typed into a database.
It’s read straight from the chain.
Signing in is one signature
in Nimiq Pay —
no account,
no password.
