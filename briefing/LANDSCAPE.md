# LANDSCAPE — Squad Stake

Researched 2026-10-21 from all 204 Cycle 1–2 submissions (github.com/nimiq/miniappscompetition-submissions,
`submission.yaml` files) and Nimiq Pay source. Cycle 3: 0 visible submissions on this date.

## Map of the space
| Cluster | Entries | Gap Squad Stake fills |
|---|---|---|
| Rotating circles | Kolo, Tanda, Ajoo, Rota, Ajo, Rosco | they rotate one pot; nobody earns; Squad Stake: no rotation, money earns |
| Shared goal | NimCircle (members **send** NIM to a goal owner), Collect (group gift link) | money leaves your wallet; Squad Stake: money never leaves, still counted |
| Habit / commitment stakes | SteadyStreak, Stash, Stakes ×2, NimStreak, Pods, NimDares | individual discipline with forfeits; Squad Stake: group goal, no forfeits |
| Staking UIs | NimiqStake, Nimjar, Validator Swipe, NimiqCafe | single-user yield; Squad Stake: staking as a social savings tool |
| Non-NIM savings / yield | Piggy (USDT vault), BALLAST (USDC on Aave) | different asset; not group-first |
| Group treasury | NColab (AI group vaults) | pooled custody; Squad Stake is non-custodial |

## House style
Winners: wallet-signature identity, server-decided state, one human story, polished mobile UI.
Nimiq Pay's own design principles (`PRODUCT.md` in its repo): friendly on entry, playful when
idle, **secure on commit**: "high-stakes actions slow down"; no confetti on a transaction confirm.

## Positioning lines
- vs NimCircle: *"Nobody holds the pot. Your share never leaves your wallet."*
- vs circles: *"No turns, no waiting for your round. Everyone's money grows the whole time."*
- vs Nimjar: *"Staking with a reason and with friends."*

## The gap in one sentence
Nobody lets a group save toward something **without handing money to one person**, while the
money **earns**, with progress **proven on-chain**.

## Prepared judge questions
- *Isn't this NimCircle?* NimCircle collects NIM into the owner's wallet. Here every member keeps custody; the squad only sees proof.
- *What stops someone leaving?* Nothing, by design (self-custody). The squad sees it immediately and honestly. Mode A shows who's still in before anyone pays.
- *Why staking?* Money waiting for a goal should earn. NIM staking is native, ~8%/yr at last check, no third party.
